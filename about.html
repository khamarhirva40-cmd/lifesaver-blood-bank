About LifeSaver Blood Bank

Who We Are

LifeSaver Blood Bank is a simple web-based platform designed to provide information about blood donation, blood availability and blood requests.

The website helps donors register themselves and allows patients or their relatives to submit blood requests during emergency situations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to encourage voluntary blood donation and make blood-related information easily accessible to people who need it.

Our Vision

Our vision is to contribute towards a reliable and efficient blood donation network where every patient can get timely support.
